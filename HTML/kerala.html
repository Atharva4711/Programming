Kerala

God's Own Country - Where Nature Reigns Supreme

Information about Kerala

Capital:

Thiruvananthapuram

Area:

38,852 square kilometers

Population:

Over 35 million people

Language:

Malayalam, Tamil, English

Overview:

Kerala is a South Indian state known for its exceptional natural beauty, high literacy rate, and quality of life. Often described as "God's Own Country," Kerala is famous for its backwaters, beaches, and lush green landscapes. The state has the highest Human Development Index among Indian states and is a major tourist destination.

Key Attractions:

  • Backwaters of Alleppey: Scenic lagoons and lakes connected by canals
  • Kochi (Cochin): Historic port city with Chinese fishing nets and colonial architecture
  • Munnar: Hill station famous for tea plantations and misty mountains
  • Thekkady: Wildlife sanctuary and spice plantation region
  • Varkala: Scenic beach with dramatic cliffs and unique rock formations
  • Wayanad: District with dense forests and tribal settlements

Famous For:

  • Backwaters and Houseboat Cruises
  • Spices (Cardamom, Pepper, Cinnamon)
  • Tea Plantations
  • High Literacy and Education
  • Ayurveda and Wellness
  • Beaches and Coconut Palms

Traditional Dishes:

Appam, Stew, Puttu, Idiyappam, Karimeen Fry, Coconut Rice, Fish Curry
