Gujarat

The Land of Mahatma Gandhi and Entrepreneurship

Information about Gujarat

Capital:

Gandhinagar

Area:

196,244 square kilometers

Population:

Over 65 million people

Language:

Gujarati, Hindi, English

Overview:

Gujarat is a western Indian state known as the cradle of India's independence movement. It is the birthplace of Mahatma Gandhi. The state is a leading industrial and commercial hub of India, with a strong entrepreneurial spirit. Gujarat is also famous for its textile industry, diamond cutting, and pharmaceutical manufacturing. The state is home to the Asiatic Lion and has beautiful beaches and temples.

Key Attractions:

  • Statue of Unity: The world's tallest statue located in Kevadiya
  • Gir National Park: Home to the endangered Asiatic Lion
  • Dwarka: Ancient pilgrimage site with historic temples
  • Somnath Temple: One of the 12 Jyotirlinga temples dedicated to Lord Shiva
  • Rann of Kutch: The world's largest salt marsh with stunning white landscape
  • Ahmedabad: Ancient city with UNESCO heritage sites and textile heritage

Famous For:

  • Statue of Unity
  • Textile Industry and Diamond Cutting
  • Pharmaceutical Manufacturing
  • Asiatic Lion Conservation
  • Salt Production in Rann of Kutch
  • Handcrafts and Embroidery

Traditional Dishes:

Dhokla, Fafda-Jalebi, Khachdi, Khandyu, Undhiyu, Thepla, Bajra Roti
