Maharashtra

The Land of Legends and Bollywood

Information about Maharashtra

Capital:

Mumbai

Area:

307,713 square kilometers

Population:

Over 125 million people

Language:

Marathi, Hindi, English

Overview:

Maharashtra is the economic powerhouse of India. It is home to Bollywood, the world's largest film industry. The state is known for its vibrant culture, historical monuments, and rapid economic development. Mumbai, the capital, is the financial hub of India and houses the headquarters of major corporations and the Reserve Bank of India.

Key Attractions:

  • Gateway of India: An iconic monument in Mumbai overlooking the Arabian Sea
  • Ajanta and Ellora Caves: UNESCO World Heritage Sites with ancient Buddhist and Hindu monuments
  • Lonavala and Khandala: Hill stations famous for natural beauty and adventure sports
  • Aurangabad: Historical city known for Mughal architecture
  • Pune: Cultural capital known as the Oxford of the East

Famous For:

  • Bollywood Film Industry
  • Financial Markets and Stock Exchange
  • Historical Monuments
  • Cotton Industry
  • IT Services
  • Vada Pav (Street Food)

Traditional Dishes:

Vada Pav, Pav Bhaji, Puran Poli, Misal Pav, Bhakri
